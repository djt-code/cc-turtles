ps: list the processes

  ps [-l] [-e] [-A]

Every process init runs (see `help init`), one a line: its process ID, terminal (con: the screen; pts/n: a telnet session; ?: none, as for a service), state (R running: ps itself, or what started it; S waiting for an event; N not yet started), the time it has spent running (seconds), and its command.  -l adds the parent's ID and when it started (the computer's clock).  -e and -A are taken and ignored: every process is always listed.  Without init there are no processes to list (only the shell this runs in), and ps says so, status 1.
