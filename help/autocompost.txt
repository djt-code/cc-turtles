autocompost: a turtle that sits on a composter and composts everything compostable you give it

Put the turtle directly on top of a composter, with a hopper under the composter to take the bone meal out.  Fill the turtle with anything: it drops each compostable item in one at a time (dropDown only ever takes one), waits while the composter is full, and sleeps until its inventory changes when there's nothing left to compost.  It never moves.

What's compostable is learned, not listed, so modded items work too: an item the composter refuses while it isn't full is not compostable. Those go into a chest in front of the turtle if there is one, otherwise they stay in the turtle.

Usage: autocompost To start it at boot:  echo "shell.run('autocompost')" >> /startup.lua
