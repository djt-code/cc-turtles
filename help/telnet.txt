telnet: a telnet client: another computer's shell or screen, here

  telnet host [port]
      (port 23)

Connects over the network stack (`ip up`) and shows the other side's screen: telnetd on another computer (a shell, or its console), or any Telnet server that draws with ANSI escapes.  What you type goes there, arrows and all; the mouse and pasting too, if the server asks.  Ctrl+T goes there as Ctrl+T.  Ctrl+] is telnet's own: then q leaves, t sends an "interrupt process" (what stops a program even on the console), any other key goes back.  It ends when the other side closes.  A screen without colour shows the colours as greys.
