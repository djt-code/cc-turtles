mine: tunnel-mining turtle for CC:Tweaked

Needs a pickaxe (any *_pickaxe upgrade), equipped or in the inventory. Any other tool is parked in the inventory while it runs and put back afterwards.  Optional: Advanced Peripherals Chunky Turtle upgrade so it keeps running unattended.

Usage:  mine [<width> <height> [length]] [--spacing N] [--no-lights] [--here]
        mine --resume
            keep digging the tunnel it stopped in (from the start, or from the last home)
        mine ... --new
            start a fresh tunnel, forgetting the old one
        mine haul
            shuttle the valuables in the homes back to the start, as many trips as it takes
        mine reset
            back to the last home it built, after a Ctrl+T or restart
        mine reset --to-start
            back to the start instead
        mine reset --here
            turtle was put back at the start by hand

width  = blocks to the RIGHT of the turtle (negative = to the LEFT)
height = blocks UP from the turtle (negative = DOWN)
length = blocks the tunnel runs ahead; leave it out to keep going until the turtle can't (out of chests or fuel, a full chest, bedrock)

With no size, it digs the one in the settings: 3x3, 32 long unless changed (`set mine.width 5`, `set mine.length 0` for no end; also mine.height, mine.spacing, mine.lights).  A size given here is saved there, so the next `mine` (or the autostart) digs the same.

The turtle's own column and row are part of the tunnel: it stands in the mouth at one bottom (or top, for negative heights) corner, and the tunnel starts with the block in front of it.  `mine 3 3 32` digs a 3x3 tunnel 32 long, up and to the right of the turtle.

Placement: put the turtle where the tunnel's corner should start, facing along it.  Put a chest directly ABOVE the start for valuables and one directly BELOW it for bulk (stone, cobblestone, deepslate, dirt, gravel, sand...).  Stock the top chest with fuel and with CHESTS: the turtle builds a new home out of two of them every `spacing` blocks. Torches in it too, and it lights the tunnel (unless --no-lights).  And a crafting table, so it can craft more chests and torches from wood it finds (see Crafting below).
